## Fresh reader failure and correction

A different original ordinary runtime launched for schematic opening. The
opening coordinator prepared its checkpoint and consumed its data token, but
the tracked execution capture refused before bridge dispatch. Its journal is
terminal `generated-navigation-not-dispatched`, with no orphan risk, no
navigation and no design effect. The consumed opening was not repeated. The
existing no-dispatch owner-release branch released its reservation without
inventing a native cleanup receipt. Normal closure and helper disposal for this
separate reader remain part of the original launcher lifecycle.

The source cause is the facade-wide engine's permanent execution-validator
binding. Its first importer capture bound the old runtime. Releasing that
runtime and selecting the new reader correctly caused the old validator to
refuse a replacement. Rebinding that validator would undermine its contract.

The generated schematic composition now constructs a separate engine and
validator for its exact already-owned reader runtime. Its tracked dispatch and
original cleanup share that engine. The index checks the same original runtime
and allocation whenever the validator selects it. The old validator remains
permanently bound and still refuses replacement; no capture, ownership or
authority is reconstructed from JSON. A focused test uses real unlaunched
runtime instances to reproduce the old binding refusal and verify the distinct
new binding, changed-index refusal and zero native calls. A fresh live opening
with the corrected composition is still required.

identify a practical cost; they do not establish a performance improvement or
