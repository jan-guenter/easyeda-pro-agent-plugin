four generated helper copies and complete ratline import. It holds the import

request/event authority directly to the successor. The handoff cannot be
recreated from copied IDs. Direct successor resume cannot bypass the held
source continuation. The same once-only worker-loaded notification is handled

matches all 75 TypeScript cases using IDs 301 through 375. The first local SDK
restore failed because an unrelated reference pack was absent; direct compilation

in private evidence.
