## Limits and remaining work

Two sequential matching projections are not an atomic or continuously observed
snapshot. They do not prove complete model membership, source membership,
startup completion, ratline/worker completion, connectivity, rendering or
persistence. Missing stateWatcher models and other object families remain
outside this result. Populated Arc/String cases, numeric-rounding edges,
failure recovery, inverse operations and writer/Save/reopen lifecycles remain
untested here. Native enumeration precedes bounds, and an observation deadline
does not cancel vendor work.

Private paths, native identities, design names and raw observations remain
outside the repository. Hashes detect changed evidence; they do not authenticate
it against a party able to rewrite the evidence store. No new runtime generation
or editing authority follows from this record.
