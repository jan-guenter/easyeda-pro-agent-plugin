## Implemented route

main/renderer factory. It cannot upgrade an older startup, preparation, route,
Combined or standalone cache owner. Published main construction precedes
project capture. Original assignments, actor and route observations then join
one checkpoint-member footprint's complete native source to its original owned
copy preparation, source assets and selected historical head.

The route retains actual opening, source and preparation references across
awaits. It accepts no caller-supplied plan, native handle, clock, command budget
or execution owner. Separate strict launch, factory, qualification and abort
journal discriminants preserve the older persisted formats. Failures consume
their stage, preserve partial observations and retain the original cleanup
owner. Normal close, original root/empty-Job termination, helper release and
record settlement remain distinct actions. Confirmed abort targets only the
original forward Job and prevents subsequent normal work.

The result keeps `executionReady:false`. It does not seal source for a closed
snapshot, verify current unlocked-head age or atomic prewrite queues, arm the
document clocks, prove backend publication or reserve the complete four-role
writer envelope. No create, copy/delete, scheduler, execution-owner or Save
route is enabled. The exhaustive inventory still lists the full copy writer's
remaining omissions. The operating procedure is in the packaged
[qualification reference](../../plugins/easyeda-pro-control/skills/easyeda-pro-control/references/current-footprint-forward-prewrite.md).
